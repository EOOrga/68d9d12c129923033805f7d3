Privacy Policy for BINET HERVE JACQUES

Effective date: May 12, 2026

BINET HERVE JACQUES is provided by BINET HERVE JACQUES. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information handled by the app
The app lets you save operations tasks, inventory records, maintenance requests, visitor entries, and local workspace preferences. This information is stored on your device using local app storage.

No account or cloud sync
BINET HERVE JACQUES does not require account registration. It does not upload operations records to a server, provide cloud sync, or sell personal information.

Data collection
The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment information, or health data.

Data sharing
The app does not share your operations records or preferences with BINET HERVE JACQUES or third parties.

Data retention and deletion
Your records remain on your device until you delete the app data or uninstall the app. Removing the app from your device removes locally stored app records according to Android system behavior.

Children
The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Security
Because operations records are stored locally, protect your device with the security features available on Android.
